FAQ

When will my package arrive?

Delivery times depend on your region and shipping method. You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order leaves our studio. Most domestic orders arrive within 3–5 business days.

Do you ship outside Sweden?

Yes. We ship within the EU and to selected countries overseas. Shipping cost and delivery time appear at checkout once you enter your address. If your country is not listed, email us and we will see what we can arrange.

Can I return or exchange something?

Unopened items in original packaging may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund or exchange. Art prints and opened paper goods are only accepted if faulty—please send photos when you contact us so we can help quickly.

Do you take custom or corporate orders?

We occasionally take small B2B runs and custom editions for local partners. Describe your timeline, quantities, and product idea in your message and we will reply with availability and next steps.
