About us

JKPG Studio is a small creative label rooted in Jönköping. We design and curate souvenirs, prints, wearables, and studio goods that celebrate the city—from Lake Vättern to the streets around Piren. Everything here is meant to feel thoughtful, local, and easy to take home or gift to someone who loves this place as much as we do.
